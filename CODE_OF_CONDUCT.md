# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be straightforward and be decent. Discuss the work, not the person.

## Expected

- Stay on the technical substance: what happened, what you expected, what would help.
- Assume the other side is acting in good faith, including when they are wrong.
- Accept a "no". Not every request fits the product, and a maintainer may decline one.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning remarks about who somebody is.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Posting other people's production data — including in a bug report. Redact it first.

## Security reports are different

Never open a public issue for a vulnerability. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); we would rather receive it privately and fix it before it is public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions, and may block an account, when this document is not respected. Deliberate abuse needs no warning first.

Concerns about someone's conduct — including a maintainer's — go to **info@yggarolite.cz**. Reports are handled privately.
